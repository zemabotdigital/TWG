## Read this before you offer a single place

I corrected the website against your Letter of Participation. Three things the
letter does **not** say, which earlier drafts of this site assumed:

**1. There is no President involved.** The letter never mentions the President
of Sri Lanka. The site no longer does either — I checked the rendered pages and
the word appears zero times.

**2. There is no Prime Minister involved.** Temple Trees is the Prime Minister's
official residence, and it is named in the letter purely as the **venue**. The
letter does not say the Prime Minister attends, hosts, or presents anything.
Describing this as a government event, or implying a meeting with either leader,
would be a claim your own invitation does not support.

**3. The invitation is addressed to Maharaja alone.** It confirms *his*
participation as an awardee. It says nothing about accompanying guests — how
many, whether they may enter Temple Trees, or on what terms.

That third point is the one to settle first. **Get written confirmation from
Anyelp Groups covering how many guests may accompany Maharaja and what access
they have, before anyone is offered a paid place.** Selling entry you have not
been granted is the kind of mistake that is very hard to undo, especially at a
head-of-government residence.
